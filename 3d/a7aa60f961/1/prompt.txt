- Tasks are **deliberately never contributed upstream**, so reference solutions stay out of training scrapes. The paper reports a sweep of upstream repos as of June 5, 2026 found no similar implementations.

### Paper
`arXiv:2607.07946`, submitted **2026-07-08**, Wenqi Huang, Charley Lee, Leonard Tng, Serena Ge (cs.SE, cs.LG). Headline methodological claim: independent LLM judge disagreed with DeepSWE's hand-written verifiers **1.4%** of the time vs **32.4%** for SWE-bench Pro's inherited tests.

## 4. Is "v1.1" a thing anywhere else in SWE-bench land?

Yes, and it's a **trap**: **`swebench` PyPI 1.1.0** was the release that moved the *evaluation harness* to containerized/sandboxed Docker execution. That is a **harness version, not a dataset version**. No `princeton-nlp/*` SWE-bench dataset has ever shipped a "v1.1". Current `swebench` package is **4.1.0** (2025-09-11) — note it has had **no release in ~11 months**.
