<result>## Shape verdict first

The sidecar-with-gz-authoritative-and-silent-fallback shape is right for a gated rollout — a new on-disk format that must prove a 3.1s win deserves a fallback, and the fidelity contract (`DeepEqual` round-trip) is the correct way to earn the right to swap later. What entrenches the two artifacts is not the sidecar itself but **the digest coupling**: because validity is defined as "SHA-256 of the gz bytes equals the one embedded here", the gz file is permanently load-bearing on the store's read path, and the store can never become the primary with the gz as a fallback *format version* — it structurally cannot outlive the thing it hashes. Finding 1 is how to keep the rollout safety without buying that.

**1. `internal/sem/search_store.go:611-628` (+ `search_cache.go:1465`) — digest coupling should be the existing generation marker**

`loadSearchStoreForEntry` calls `entry.digest()`, which `io.Copy`s the whole 6.5MB gz through SHA-256 on every gated load.
